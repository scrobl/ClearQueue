# Privacy Policy for Clear Queue

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Clear Queue does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code.

## What the app does with your information

- It reads the song that is playing (title, artist, album and artwork) from the Music app on your device, only to show it on screen and to rebuild the queue. This information never leaves your device.
- It stores your settings (whether to clear automatically, and which app to open afterwards) on your device only.
- If you choose to open another app after clearing, Clear Queue opens that app's link. It sends no information to it.

## Your control

You can revoke Media & Apple Music access at any time in Settings > Clear Queue. Deleting the app removes its settings.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/scrobl/ClearQueue/issues.
